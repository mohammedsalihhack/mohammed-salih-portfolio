About Me

Cybersecurity Student & Researcher

I am a Cybersecurity Student & Researcher interested in defensive security, Security Operations Center (SOC) practices and Blue Team operations.


My learning approach is hands-on. I work with security labs, Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges, digital forensics, security analysis and Linux-based security tooling to understand how attacks work and how defenders can detect and investigate them.


I am particularly interested in understanding security events from a defender's perspective: collecting evidence, analyzing artifacts, identifying suspicious behavior and documenting findings clearly.

Areas of Focus

Topics I'm actively developing.

SOC

Log analysis, security monitoring, alert investigation and understanding incident-response workflows.

Blue Team

Defensive security, detection concepts, endpoint visibility and security event analysis.

Forensics

File analysis, artifact investigation, evidence extraction and technical documentation.

Learning Philosophy

I focus on understanding the reasoning behind security tools rather than simply running commands. Each project and challenge is an opportunity to understand what happened, why it happened and how it could be detected or prevented.
